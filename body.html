📖 How to play

  1. Pass the phone round. Everyone taps the pack to see their own secret word — keep it hidden!
  2. One player is the imposter. They don't get the word, they get a clue about it instead.
  3. Take turns saying one word about your secret word. Never say the word itself.
  4. Go round as many times as you chose, then everyone votes.
  5. Stuck as the imposter? Tap I need a bigger clue on your card. Everyone finds out afterwards.
  6. Detectives win if they catch the imposter. Imposter wins if they get away — or if they're caught but can still guess the secret word.

POINTS

+1 — you voted for an imposter
+2 — you were the imposter and got away with it
+1 — you were caught, but guessed the secret word

GOOD CLUES / BAD CLUES

Secret word is Goalkeeper. A good clue is “gloves”. A rubbish clue is “football” — it's so obvious the imposter can just copy it.

✏️ Our own words

One per line, like this:

GRANDMA'S DOG | A pet somebody in this room knows

The bit after the | is the clue the imposter gets. Keep it clear but don't give it away.
